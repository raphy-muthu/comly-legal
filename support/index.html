Safety

If anyone is in danger, call 911 first. Comly is not an emergency service.

To report a listing, open it in the app and choose Report listing. To report a person, open their profile and choose Report this profile. Reports go to the Comly moderation team. Profile → Safety center in the app explains how safety labels and reports work.

Common questions

How does payment work?

Comly is a matchmaking app. Pay is agreed between neighbors and handled outside the app, by cash, Venmo or whatever works for both of you. Comly never handles money and takes no cut.

How do I contact a helper or a job poster?

Phone numbers and exact addresses stay hidden until the poster accepts a helper's application. Then contact details unlock for both of them.

Can teens use Comly?

Yes. Users must be 13 or older, and users under 18 need a parent or guardian's agreement to the Terms of Service. Every job shows a safety label, and teens can only apply to jobs suitable for their age. A parent or guardian can approve a teen by email, which unlocks more job types.

Why does a job say "You can't apply"?

The job's safety label requires an older helper, or a parent's approval you don't have yet. The card explains which one.

Why was my job labeled "Caution"?

Comly's safety review flags weather, physical effort or equipment so helpers know what to expect. If you think a label is wrong, tap Request review on the job.

Why can't I post another job?

You can have up to 3 active listings at a time. Mark one as filled or completed, cancel it, or delete it to post another. Paused listings still count.

What happens if someone doesn't show up?

Open the job and choose Report a no-show. A person reviews every report before a strike is applied, and a confirmed strike can be reversed on appeal. Three confirmed strikes can suspend an account.

When can I leave a review?

After both sides confirm the job is done. The poster marks it complete, the helper confirms, and then a Leave a Review button appears for both of you.

How do I delete a listing?

Open your job, tap the ••• menu, and choose Delete.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. If you can't sign in, see Delete your account.
