The safety engine

Every job is checked before anyone sees it.

When a job is posted, Comly reads it, flags hazards, and gives it a safety tier. A teen can only apply to jobs suited to their age. Work that isn't allowed is never listed.

AI review checks posts for risk, if you allow it.
Hazard rules are enforced by the server, so they can't be skipped.

Built different

Trust, engineered in.

Private by default

Phone numbers and exact addresses stay hidden until a poster accepts a specific helper. There's no way to message a teen out of the blue.

Parents stay in the loop

Helpers under 18 can ask a parent or guardian to approve them by email, with no extra account. Some jobs, like babysitting, require it.

Fair pay, no fees

AI suggests a fair price and shows local minimum-wage guidance. You settle pay directly.

Real accountability

Verified profiles, reviews from both sides, and no-show reports a person reviews.
