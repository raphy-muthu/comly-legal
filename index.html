Comly connects neighbors who need small jobs done with trusted local helpers, including teens looking for their first work.

Safety-checked jobs

Every job gets a safety label before helpers see it. Teens can only apply to work suited to their age, and work that isn't allowed is never listed.

Your number stays private

Phone numbers and exact addresses stay hidden until a poster accepts a specific helper for a specific job.

Parents stay in the loop

Helpers under 18 can ask a parent or guardian to approve them by email. Some jobs, like babysitting, require it.

Fair pay, no fees

Comly suggests a fair price, then you and your helper agree on pay and settle it directly.

For everyone on the block

Need help? Post a job in under a minute, compare applicants by their reviews and verification, and accept the one you trust.

Teens: find paid work you can walk to, and build a reputation with every job you finish.

Seniors: Simple Mode has bigger buttons, preset tasks and fewer steps.
